Shared nine-stage workflow

01 · Welcome & accessibilitySetup mode, accessibility and keyboard-first navigation.
02 · Language & keyboardLocale, input languages, keyboard layout and timezone proposal.
03 · Hardware & firmwareArchitecture and BIOS/UEFI choice; hosted preview does not probe host hardware.
04 · Network & sourceLocal ISO, HTTPS/NFS source or offline plan.
05 · System role & desktopDesktop, server, minimal, developer and Aurora/Marine selections.
06 · StorageGuided/custom proposal, filesystem, encryption and OS-preservation intent.
07 · Accounts & securityHostname, admin account and firewall policy proposal.
08 · BootloaderSpit Fire → Jasper/GRUB plan and firmware-specific review.
09 · Review & commitSummarize the plan; native commit remains blocked pending transaction support.
