Remaining implementation and acceptance gates

  1. AP startup and measured SMP: implement real AP trampoline/startup, per-CPU initialization and interrupt/IPI routing; demonstrate bounded concurrent execution on at least two CPUs. The separate Chimera #321 QEMU boot matrix failed at AP startup.
  2. Architecture scheduling: implement architecture context save/restore, independent guarded stacks, first-run entry and timer-driven preemption. Targeted CI tests are not a substitute for full runtime proof.
  3. Persistent storage and VFS: implement block-device discovery/I/O, a general VFS and a persistent filesystem; verify write, sync, reboot and read-back durability.
  4. Kernel-owned PID 1 and process lifecycle: implement a resident supervised init, address-space/process lifecycle, child create/exec/wait/reap, service dependency startup and restart.
  5. Installed-root handoff: implement and verify a durable switch from the bootstrap initramfs to the installed root.
  6. Installer and recovery: implement disk-writing transactions, validation, rollback and actual filesystem repair with postcondition checks.
  7. Native desktops: demonstrate complete Aurora, Marine, FreeXP and FreeWin11 sessions with real graphics, input, compositor and process lifecycle traces.
  8. Command, ISA and Windows compatibility: provide genuine implementations or verified providers, provenance/licensing and semantic conformance tests; do not count catalogue entries or stubs as implementations.
  9. Six-case production certification: authenticate Live, Installer and Recovery under BIOS and UEFI on the same immutable ISO, with firmware/QEMU traces, ISO and init hashes, storage/installer/recovery postconditions and measured SMP evidence.
  10. Production ISO publication: publish only after every preceding gate passes and the acceptance evidence is independently reviewed.
